# This is your notes

Welcome to MindfulOpus, a wellness and coaching business for classical musicians. I’m Joanna Zakany, but you can call me Jo! I am a violist with The Cleveland Orchestra, Professor at Kent State University, Certified yoga Instructor and Duke-Trained Well-being Coach.

I created this business as a way to help others in the classical musical world find more balance and ease in their lives. My specific background offers a true yin-yang approach; one that helps clients thrive under pressure and work for their goals in a mindful way, while also balancing the musical journey with more wellness and an integrative approach to life. 

 I offer clients support in many ways, including:

Performance-related anxiety
Safe and musician-geared yoga sessions
Burnout support and prevention
Meditation instruction
Pre-audition preparation
Mindfulness and well-being coaching

At MindfulOpus, our philosophy is:

Music isn’t WHO you are, it’s WHAT you do.
HOW you are at WHAT you do becomes…

WHO YOU ARE.

You can choose HOW you live your life one piece at at time, at any time of your life. If you’re interested in finding out more, please reach out for a complimentary conversation!
