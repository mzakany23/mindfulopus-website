Privacy

Last updated October 3, 2026

This page explains how MindfulOpus handles information from people who visit mindfulopus.com, sign up for our emails or book a session. Questions go to contact@mindfulopus.com.

Visiting the website

We use analytics cookies to understand how the site is used, such as which pages are visited, how people found us and their general location. We use this only to improve our own website. We don't sell it or use it for advertising.

Signing up and booking

If you sign up for our emails or book a session, we use your details only to provide what you asked for. Payments are handled by our payment provider; we never see your card details. You can unsubscribe from our emails at any time.

Our YouTube channel

MindfulOpus uses its own app, through YouTube API Services, to upload and manage videos on the MindfulOpus YouTube channel. It works only with our channel and does not access information about viewers or anyone else's account. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked from your Google Account's security settings.

Your choices

You can block cookies in your browser; the site works without them. To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, email contact@mindfulopus.com.
