What is StudentOpus?

StudentOpus is live group coaching created specifically for college and conservatory music students. Each month centers around one topic, with room for real questions, coaching, discussion, and learning from what other students are experiencing too.

  • Performance anxiety
  • Practice-room overwhelm
  • Burnout
  • Confidence
  • Inner critic
  • Schedules & balance
  • Auditions
  • Life as a creative musician

FAQ

Do I have to be coached?

No. You’re welcome to bring a question, but you can also simply listen and learn.

Do cameras have to be on?

Cameras on is encouraged because it helps the room feel connected, but you’re still welcome if you need yours off.

Will I definitely get coached?

Not necessarily. Depending on the size of the group and the flow of the conversation, I may not be able to coach every student individually. You’ll still be able to learn from the coaching, questions, and discussion happening in the room.

Is this a membership?

No. StudentOpus is completely drop-in. There’s no subscription and no ongoing commitment.

Come as you are. Bring what’s actually going on.
