Privacy

Last updated October 3, 2026

MindfulOpus is Jo Zakany's coaching practice for musicians. This page explains what information we collect through mindfulopus.com, our sign-up forms, StudentOpus payments and our YouTube channel, and what we do with it. Questions go to contact@mindfulopus.com.

When you visit the website

We use PostHog and Google Analytics to understand how the site is used, so we can make it more useful. They record things like:

  • which pages you view, how far you scroll and which buttons you click;
  • the website or link that brought you here;
  • your approximate location (city and country), worked out from your internet address;
  • the kind of device and browser you use.

Both tools set cookies in your browser so they can tell repeat visits apart. We use this information only to understand our own website. We don't sell it, and we don't use it for advertising. Google Analytics is provided by Google; see Google's Privacy Policy.

When you sign up

The free Well-Being Wheel workbook and the StudentOpus interest list use forms run by Brevo. You give us your name and email address, and we use them to send what you asked for and occasional emails from Jo. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can ask us to remove you at any time.

When you pay for StudentOpus

Payments are handled by Stripe. Your card details go to Stripe, not to us; we receive Stripe's record of the payment. So we can tell which visits lead to a booking, the Reserve link passes Stripe an anonymous visit code, not your name or contact details. After paying, you register for your session with Zoom, which handles that registration.

Our YouTube uploader

MindfulOpus uses a small app of its own, the MindfulOpus Podcast Uploader, to upload podcast episodes and Shorts to the MindfulOpus YouTube channel and to update their titles, descriptions and thumbnails. It uses YouTube API Services and works only with the MindfulOpus channel it is connected to. It does not access anyone else's YouTube account or any information about viewers.

Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. MindfulOpus's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account's security settings.

Your choices

  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser's settings; the site works without them.
  • You can email contact@mindfulopus.com to ask what we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted.

Changes

If we change how we handle information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
