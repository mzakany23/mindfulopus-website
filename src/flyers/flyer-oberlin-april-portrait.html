Part 1 February 16, 2026
Excellence Over Perfection
Learn the signature MindfulOpus framework that helps you keep your musical high-standards and drive, without the burden and exhaustion of perfection—all so you can find more joy and enthusiasm in your artistic work.
  • A clear distinction between excellence and perfection (and why it matters)
  • Tools to practice with more focus, efficiency, and emotional steadiness
  • A way to recover faster from mistakes and stay engaged instead of shutting down
Part 2 March 16, 2026 · 5:30–6:30 pm · Bibbins 238
From Inner-Critic to Inner-Coach
Learn how to cultivate an inner-coach that offers you refuge from the pain of beating yourself up, and allows instead for the freedom of supporting yourself towards your goals.
  • Distinguish between critical and constructive self-talk
  • Interrupt negative self-talk in real time
  • Cultivate an inner coach that keeps you grounded, motivated, and self-respecting—even under pressure
Part 3 April 20, 2026 · 5:30–6:30 pm · Bibbins 238
Your Personalized Performance Process
Explore the MindfulOpus Performance Process, a flexible framework designed to help you understand what actually works best for you.
  • Greater clarity around your unique strengths and tendencies
  • A repeatable process you can adapt for juries, auditions, recitals, and beyond
  • Build in a sense of work-life balance, and tend to your well-being
  • More clarity in how you prepare—so you feel more confident in your performance
